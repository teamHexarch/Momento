Momento Content Standard

  1. What is shown: how much of the body is exposed.
  2. What is happening: pose, gesture, touching, acts. This can raise a clothed photo to 100.
  3. Context: beach, sport, art, medical, breastfeeding can make exposure normal, never sexual activity.
  4. Choose the highest level that applies. Torn between two? Choose the higher.

May be under 18: nude, sexual or sexualised in any way, at any level: keep private, tick May be under 18, and escalate to the Momento team at once. Never download or share it.

No consent: hidden camera, upskirt or “revenge” images: keep private, tick Other, escalate.

Art follows the same levels. Context can justify level 75 art; sexual activity in art is still 100.
