How it works

From the first photo to the album you keep, in five steps.

Features

Only the people
who were there

Every Momento album has a sharing zone. Guests can join only while they’re inside it, so a wedding album never fills with strangers’ photos.

150 m

4 of 9 guests can join

Your people, in one place

Start a group for the friends you share with most. Everyone in it sees every album shared with it, and new people join with an invite link, QR code or album code.

Events near you

Scan the map for public events happening around you, or search any town. Turn on alerts and Momento tells you when one starts nearby.

Keep the ones you love

Bookmark your favourite photos and save albums you want to come back to. They all wait on the Bookmarked tab in My albums.
